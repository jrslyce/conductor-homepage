FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

Don't see yours? Ask it on the call.

What is a fractional AI officer?

A senior AI advisor who works with your business part-time. You get strategy, oversight and hands-on help without the cost of a full-time executive.

Will you try to sell us AI we don't need?

No. Our job is to find where AI adds real value and to say so when it doesn't. If the right answer is a better spreadsheet or a process change, that's what we'll recommend.

We're small. Is this for us?

Yes. We work best with businesses of 10 to 250 people that want to use AI but don't have anyone to lead it.

Will our data be used to train AI models?

No. We only approve tools and plans that contractually exclude your data from training, and our guardrail layer keeps sensitive details from being sent in the first place.

Our team already uses ChatGPT. Do we have to stop?

Usually not. We'll move you onto business accounts with the right settings, write a clear policy, and show people what's safe to share.

What exactly is the Orchestrator?

Our method and platform for turning prototypes and vibe-coded apps into secure, hosted, maintainable software, connected to your data guardrails.

How fast will we see results?

Most clients have a roadmap within a month and their first working project within 60–90 days.

What if we decide AI isn't right for us yet?

Then the assessment has done its job. You'll know where you stand, what risks exist today, and what to watch for.
